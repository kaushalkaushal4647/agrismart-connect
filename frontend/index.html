Architecture

The Demand-First Marketplace

Our dynamic engine eliminates the risk of unsold inventory by pairing produce to verified buyers before harvesting begins.

01
🌾

Farmer Lists Produce

Farmers submit crop variety, expected yield (kg), minimum price, harvest date, and GPS coordinates.

02
🛒

Buyers Create Demand

Consumers, restaurants, and retailers post specific quantity requirements and preferred delivery dates.

03
⚡

Dynamic Matching

The matching algorithm computes optimal pairs based on harvest timing, proximity, price, and quality grade.

04
🏢

Hub Quality Check

Produce arrives at regional collection hubs for digital weighing, physical grading, sorting, and packing.

05
🚚

Dispatch & Payout

Delivery partners complete the final leg while automated split payouts disburse fair earnings to farmers.
